The Gulf · Lead story

Iran hits back at Gulf bases as US–Iran clash widens

A day after US jets struck an Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island, Iran said it targeted US drone and missile sites at Kuwait’s Ali Al Salem base. Kuwait said its air defences were countering hostile drones, and Bahrain said it intercepted Iranian aerial attacks for a third time this week. No damage was reported.

Iran’s allies fired toward Saudi targets as well; debris from an attempted attack was reported to have damaged an Aramco tower in Riyadh.

Ukraine

Kerch Bridge hit again on a second straight day

Ukraine says a second day of strikes collapsed part of the bridge’s road span. Russia confirmed the damage and says it will rebuild; trains have resumed and cars are being sent to ferries.

The fuel war

Ukrainian drone strikes since late October have cut Russian crude processing by about 45%. By today, 15 of Russia’s 35 largest refineries were damaged or halted, up from nine a week earlier.

Ukraine · Diplomacy

Talks resume in Abu Dhabi as sides agree biggest prisoner swap

Trilateral Ukraine–US–Russia talks resumed after an initial agreement on a prisoner exchange, with the largest swap of prisoners of war agreed. President Zelenskyy said the Coalition of the Willing meets tomorrow, with new support packages being prepared.

Asia · Summit

The APEC summit opened in Shenzhen. The White House says Trump, Xi and Putin will hold their first three-way meeting at the summit; Xi and Putin met ahead of it, and Trump arrived in Manila for QUAD talks before China.

Washington

Peltola flips Alaska, sealing a 56–44 Senate majority

Democrat Mary Peltola defeated Republican Dan Sullivan in Alaska, the final race of the 2026 midterms and the ninth Democratic flip. She becomes the first Alaska Native in the US Senate.

Climate · COP31

Draft calling for fossil-fuel phaseout splits the talks

An 18-page draft circulated by co-host Australia, calling for a fossil-fuel phaseout and net zero in wealthy countries by 2030, divided nations at the climate talks in Antalya, Turkey. India called for climate justice and finance.

Gaza

Ceasefire could collapse, peace board warns

The US-backed Board of Peace warned the UN Security Council that the year-old Gaza ceasefire could collapse unless Israel and Hamas fulfil their 2025 commitments. Australia and Britain announced coordinated sanctions on Israeli settlement expansion, and President Herzog tapped Gadi Eisenkot to form Israel’s government.

Asia

Cyclone Akvan bears down on Andhra Pradesh

The storm intensified over the Bay of Bengal. India’s weather office forecasts landfall in Andhra Pradesh on 20 November, with winds up to 145 km/h.

Markets

Nvidia board says no to Huang’s return

The board rejected Jensen Huang’s offer to return as chief executive after a record quarter.
