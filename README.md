One newspaper front page that sums up Wednesday 18 November 2026, built for
make.fun's [news-image-20261118](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118)
bounty. The picture is `entry.png` (1024x1024); `main.ts` copies it to the
grader's output folder. The page itself was laid out in `design/index.html`
and rendered to a screenshot with headless Chromium.

## The stories on the page

- **Lead, the Gulf:** Iran hits back at Gulf bases — a day after US jets hit
  a missile site on Qeshm Island, Iran said it targeted US sites at Kuwait's
  Ali Al Salem base; Kuwait's air defences countered hostile drones and
  Bahrain intercepted Iranian attacks for a third time this week; no damage
  reported. Debris from an attempted attack was reported to have damaged an
  Aramco tower in Riyadh.
- **Ukraine:** Kerch Bridge hit again — a second straight day of strikes
  collapsed part of the road span; Russia confirmed the damage and says it
  will rebuild; trains resumed, cars go by ferry. Ukrainian drone strikes
  have cut Russian crude processing by about 45%; 15 of the 35 largest
  refineries are damaged or halted.
- **Ukraine, diplomacy:** Talks resume in Abu Dhabi — trilateral
  Ukraine–US–Russia talks restarted after a prisoner-exchange agreement, with
  the largest POW swap agreed; Zelenskyy said the Coalition of the Willing
  meets tomorrow.
- **Asia:** APEC opens in Shenzhen — the White House says Trump, Xi and Putin
  will hold their first three-way meeting at the summit; Xi and Putin met
  ahead of it, and Trump arrived in Manila for QUAD talks.
- **Washington:** Peltola flips Alaska — Democrat Mary Peltola beat
  Republican Dan Sullivan, sealing a 56–44 Senate majority, the final race of
  the 2026 midterms and the ninth Democratic flip; first Alaska Native in the
  US Senate.
- **Climate:** COP31 draft splits the talks — an 18-page draft from co-host
  Australia calling for a fossil-fuel phaseout and net zero in wealthy
  countries by 2030 divided nations in Antalya; India called for climate
  justice and finance.
- **Gaza:** Ceasefire could collapse — the US-backed Board of Peace warned
  the UN Security Council; Australia and Britain announced coordinated
  sanctions on Israeli settlement expansion; Herzog tapped Gadi Eisenkot to
  form Israel's government.
- **Asia:** Cyclone Akvan bears down on Andhra Pradesh — landfall forecast
  for 20 November, winds up to 145 km/h.
- **Markets:** Nvidia board says no to Huang's return as CEO after a record
  quarter.
- **Also today:** Reported — a third US carrier reached the Middle East;
  Rubio ruled out a cyberattack in the NSA and Apple outages.

Every story on the page happened or was first reported on 18 November 2026.
